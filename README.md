# Stay Pass QR Studio

Open-source, non-commercial studio for minting a **generic Open Stay Pass**, a **HostCasa arrival**, or a **Folios proof**.

Live on Netlify: [stay-pass-qr-studio.netlify.app](https://stay-pass-qr-studio.netlify.app)

Parent project: [FriskyDevelopments/open-stay-pass](https://github.com/FriskyDevelopments/open-stay-pass) (MIT)

## What it is

Type a stay link or message. The ticket and QR update live. House marks sit in the center of the code:

- Open Stay Pass — perforated credential + aqua pip
- HostCasa — Horizonte (ring, roof, sun, sea)
- Folios — marca maestra F + verification dot

Guests stay on the simple path. Colors, size, and error correction live under **Advanced settings**.

This studio does not issue signed credentials, sell hosting, or provide commercial support. Operator-signed passes still come from the Open Stay Pass console.
