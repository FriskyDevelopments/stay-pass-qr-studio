02 / Wallet

The same URL. A Wallet pass.

A visual carrier. Not a signed .pkpass. Not a Google save.

Apple Wallet and Google Wallet carry the same public destination as the QR and an NDEF tag. Official issuance needs Apple certificates and a Google Wallet issuer on the self-hosted credential server. This preview stays in the browser.
